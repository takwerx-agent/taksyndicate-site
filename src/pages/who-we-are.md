# WHO WE ARE...

The word syndicate is defined as a group of individuals or organizations combined to promote some common interest. 

Our common interest is the Team Awareness Kit. 

The TAK Syndicate is an ad-hoc group of focused professionals collaborating to promote the use of TAK as a tool to enhance efficiency, effectiveness and overall safety for emergency response.
